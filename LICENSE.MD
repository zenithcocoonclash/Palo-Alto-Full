## 🔧 Troubleshooting

**Download does not start** — open https://mylunora.art in a normal browser window and click **Download ZIP** again.

**Archive asks for a password** — copy it from the yellow password box on **mylunora.art**.

**App opens but tools are grayed out** — restart once after install; the first launch may finish background registration.

Connection drops — switch protocol or server region and disable conflicting VPN adapters.
